🌟 Our Ministry Heart

Heavenly Tales is born from Luke 4:18 — the moment Jesus stood in the synagogue and declared His mission. We believe that mission reaches into every home, every family, every bedtime. Good news belongs in the nursery. Liberty belongs in the hearts of our children.

This app exists because busy moms deserve a resource that's warm, beautiful, biblical, and ready every single night. You shouldn't have to search Pinterest for 20 minutes before bed. The story should be waiting for you.

📖 What Heavenly Tales Is

Every night at midnight, a new Bible bedtime story is ready — generated thoughtfully using AI, grounded in Scripture, and tailored to your child's age.

Three reading levels meet your child where they are:

  • 🌱 Tiny Ones (2–4) — Simple words, gentle repetition, the joy of God's love.
  • 🔍 Explorers (5–8) — Adventure, courage, and learning to trust.
  • ⭐ Seekers (9–12) — Deeper questions, real faith, lasting truth.

Each story includes a faith lesson, parent prayer points, biblical context, and a conversation starter — everything you need for a meaningful bedtime moment.

💛 For Every Mom

We built this for the mom who's exhausted but doesn't want to skip the sacred. For the mom who wants her child to grow up knowing these stories in their bones. For the mom who prays over her children in the dark after they've fallen asleep.

You are not alone. This app stands beside you every night of the year.
